    Dairy farming is a branch of agriculture focused on long-term milk production from livestock like cows and buffaloes.
    Key components include choosing the right animal breed, managing daily feeding and health, and proper milk storage.

Choosing Animals and Breeds

Cows: Holstein-Friesian (high milk volume) and Jersey (high butterfat).
Buffaloes: Popular in regions like India and Egypt for rich, high-fat milk.
Goats & Sheep: Good for smaller spaces or niche dairy products
